## Research integrity and limitations

The work reports a developer-run implementation and internal regression record. Pass counts are not external benchmarks. The experience-retrieval bridge had not been proven to deliver controlled cross-session learning improvement; the document-source authority study includes a known end-to-end counterexample. Proposed protocols and ablations should not be represented as completed controlled experiments. Individual manuscripts identify their own outstanding evidence needs.

These texts intentionally avoid private Clair V4 source code, credentials, private regression fixtures, and proprietary routing thresholds. This public release nevertheless constitutes public disclosure; the author should seek advice about intellectual-property timing before sharing further enabling detail.

**Authorship correction:** *Beyond Memory: A Transactional Continuity Kernel for Long-Lived AI Agents* (arXiv:2608.11632) is third-party work by Jun He and Deying Yu, not a Project Clair paper.
